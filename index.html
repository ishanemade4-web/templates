🛡️ Online Safety Tips

🔑 Protect Your Password

Never share your password with anyone.

🔗 Be Careful With Links

Do not click unknown or suspicious links.

📱 Never Share OTP

Never share your OTP, PIN or banking details with anyone.

💳 Stay Safe Online

Check the website and sender before making online payments.

⚠️ Think Before You Click

Stop, check and think before clicking a link, downloading a file or sharing personal information.

🚨 Report a Scam

What to Do If You Face a Scam

1️⃣ Do not share OTP, PIN or password.

2️⃣ Stop communicating with the suspicious person.

3️⃣ Save screenshots or other evidence.

4️⃣ Inform a trusted adult, bank or relevant service.

5️⃣ In India, you can report cybercrime through the official cybercrime reporting system.

📘 About This Project
